# The 10-Slide Pitch Framework

A detailed breakdown of every slide in a venture capital or strategic investment deck.

---

## Slide 1: Company Purpose
- **Headline:** Company Name + Tagline.
- **The One-Liner:** "We build [X] for [Target Customer] that reduces [Pain] by [Metric]."
  - *Example:* "Stripe: Financial infrastructure for the internet."
  - *Example:* "Airbnb: Book rooms with locals, rather than hotels."
- **Presenter Info:** Founder names, contact details, date.

---

## Slide 2: Problem
- **The Core Pain:** State the acute friction experienced by the customer today.
- **Quantification:** How much time, money, or efficiency is lost due to this problem?
- **Current Alternatives:** What do they use right now? (e.g. "Clunky Excel sheets, legacy on-premise software, manual offshore labor").

---

## Slide 3: Solution
- **The Breakthrough:** State the core value proposition in 1 sentence.
- **Before vs. After:** How the customer's life changes immediately upon adoption.
- **Key Differentiation:** The 10x improvement factor (10x faster, 10x cheaper, or 10x simpler).

---

## Slide 4: Why Now?
- **Market Catalysts:** Identify 2–3 inflection points that make today the exact right timing:
  - Regulatory shifts (e.g., GDPR, open banking).
  - Technological shifts (e.g., local LLM inference, edge compute).
  - Cultural / Consumer behavioral shifts (e.g., remote work, creator economy).

---

## Slide 5: Market Size (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Total annual revenue opportunity if you captured 100% of the entire macro category.
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** The segment within geographic and product reach.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** Your realistic target market share in the next 3–5 years.
- *Calculation Tip:* Prefer bottom-up calculations (Number of potential buyers × average annual deal size) over generic top-down analyst reports.

---

## Slide 6: Product Architecture & Flow
- **Visual Diagram:** 3-step pipeline showing how the platform operates under the hood.
- **Secret Sauce:** Proprietary algorithms, IP, integration hooks, or proprietary datasets.

---

## Slide 7: Traction & Proof
- **Core Numbers:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), user growth rate.
- **Retention & Engagement:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR), churn rate, Daily Active Users (DAU/MAU).
- **Logos / Social Proof:** Pilot customers, enterprise contracts, or notable design partners.

---

## Slide 8: Business Model & Unit Economics
- **Monetization:** Subscription (SaaS tiers), usage-based, marketplace rake %, or enterprise licenses.
- **Unit Economics:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), LTV:CAC ratio (target > 3:1), Payback period (target < 12 months).

---

## Slide 9: Competitive Landscape & Moat
- **Competitive Grid:** Comparison across 4–5 architectural features against direct and indirect competitors.
- **Defensibility:** Network effects, high switching costs, proprietary data flywheel, or developer lock-in.

---

## Slide 10: Team & The Ask
- **Founders & Key Hires:** Previous exits, relevant domain pedigree (e.g. "Ex-Google AI lead, 10 yrs building distributed databases").
- **The Ask:**
  - Target amount (e.g., "\$2,000,000 Seed Round").
  - Runway (e.g., "18 months of runway").
  - Use of Funds: 70% Engineering & Product, 20% Go-To-Market, 10% Operations.
  - Concrete Milestones to reach the next milestone round.
