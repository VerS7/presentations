# Financials & Pitch Deck Metrics

Guidelines for structuring startup numbers and investor metric slides.

---

## 1. Core SaaS & Business Metrics Catalog

| Metric | Full Name | Healthy Benchmark | How to Display |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MRR / ARR** | Monthly / Annual Recurring Revenue | >15% MoM for early-stage | Bold stat with MoM trend badge |
| **NRR** | Net Revenue Retention | >110% (Enterprise: >120%) | Card with expansion breakdown |
| **LTV : CAC** | Lifetime Value to Acq Cost | >3 : 1 ratio | Stat box or comparison bar |
| **CAC Payback** | Months to recoup CAC | <12 months (Top tier: <6m) | Number callout |
| **Gross Margin** | Revenue minus Cost of Goods | >75% for Pure Software | Percentage card |
| **Burn Multiple** | Net Burn / Net New ARR | <1.5x (under 1.0x is elite) | Runway sustainability metric |

## 2. Market Sizing Guidelines (Bottom-Up Math)

Avoid citing only "Gartner says this is a \$50B market." Investors want to see how you calculate your market from first principles:

```text
Bottom-Up Market Formula:
TAM = [Total number of potential enterprise clients globally] × [Average Contract Value (ACV)]
SAM = [Clients matching our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) in target regions] × [ACV]
SOM = [Realistic target clients we can capture in 3 years] × [ACV]
```

### Example:
- **Total mid-market logistics companies:** 120,000 globally.
- **ACV:** \$24,000 / year.
- **TAM:** 120,000 × \$24,000 = **\$2.88 Billion**.
- **SAM (US & EU with modern ERP):** 35,000 × \$24,000 = **\$840 Million**.
- **SOM (3% market penetration over 36 months):** 1,050 clients × \$24,000 = **\$25.2 Million ARR**.

## 3. High-Impact Slide Formats

- **The Metric Triad:** Group 3 key metrics horizontally (e.g., ARR Growth, Gross Margin, Net Churn).
- **The Milestones Stepper:** Align the funding ask directly to concrete ARR goals:
  - Seed: \$0 to \$1M ARR (Product-market fit).
  - Series A: \$1M to \$5M ARR (Repeatable sales engine).
  - Series B: \$5M to \$20M ARR (International and multi-product scaling).
