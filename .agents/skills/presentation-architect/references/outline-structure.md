# Outline Structure & Slide Archetypes

An outline organizes slides into an intentional cognitive sequence. Each slide must perform a distinct communicative role.

---

## 1. Standard Deck Structure (10–12 Slides)

```text
Slide 01: Title & Hook
Slide 02: Executive Summary (The conclusion up front)
Slide 03: The Problem / Friction (Pain points with quantifiable cost)
Slide 04: The Market Catalyst / Why Now (Why previous attempts failed or what changed)
Slide 05: The Solution & Value Proposition (The core insight)
Slide 06: Architecture / How It Works (3-step flow or system diagram)
Slide 07: Proof & Metrics (Key traction, benchmark data, or ROI stats)
Slide 08: Competitive Moat / Comparison (Why our approach wins)
Slide 09: Implementation Roadmap (Next 3–4 milestones)
Slide 10: The Ask / Conclusion / Q&A
```

---

## 2. Catalog of Slide Archetypes

| Archetype | Primary Function | Ideal Layout Component |
|---|---|---|
| **Title / Cover** | Establish credibility and topic in 3 seconds | High contrast dark or brand background, subtitle, author |
| **Big Statement** | Shift perspective or introduce core thesis | 44pt+ bold text, minimal visual noise, 1 focal point |
| **Problem / Pain** | Evoke empathy and urgency | 2–3 cards with pain points, friction icons, metric callout |
| **Solution / Architecture** | Show how the system delivers results | 3-column process cards or clean pipeline diagram |
| **Metrics / KPI** | Validate claims with hard numbers | 3–4 large stat boxes (e.g. "3.5x", "\$4.2M", "99.9%") |
| **Comparison Matrix** | Demonstrate superiority over status quo | 2-column "Traditional vs. Modern" or feature check table |
| **Timeline / Roadmap** | Show structured forward momentum | Horizontal milestone stepper (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4) |
| **Case Study / Quote** | Provide social proof and authority | Big quote in quotation marks with photo/avatar & title |
| **Team / Credentials** | Establish domain authority | 3–4 profile cards with names, roles, former pedigree |
| **Summary / CTA** | Reiterate core takeaway and give next step | Bulleted recap + bold action button or contact info |

---

## 3. Rhythm and Pacing Rules

- **Contrast Pacing (Loud vs. Quiet):** Do not put three dense data slides in a row. Alternate between high-density slides (tables, metrics) and low-density breathing slides (big statements, quotes, section breaks).
- **One Focal Point per Slide:** If a slide has both a chart, a table, 5 bullets, and an architecture diagram, the audience's eyes wander and they stop listening to the speaker. Split it into two slides.
- **Progress Tracking:** In decks longer than 15 slides, use consistent section dividers or header tracker tags so the audience knows where they are in the narrative.
